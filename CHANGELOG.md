# 1.0.0

Soak armor in a dye cauldron, then hang it on a drying rack.

Craft a Dye Cauldron from a vanilla cauldron, crafting table and iron ingot. Its interface accepts one supported armor piece, one dye and one water bucket. Take the wet result, then right-click a drying rack with it. After 200 game ticks, normally ten seconds, its color is applied to the item and worn armor. Right-click the rack empty-handed to retrieve the item. Durability, enchantments, trims and custom names are preserved. Supports all 29 vanilla humanoid armor pieces and all 16 dyes. Drying progress and the hanging item persist in the world. Breaking the rack returns its item.
